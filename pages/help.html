📖 Quick Start Guide

1

Create Account

Register with your university email and set your gaming ID.

2

Create a Team

Go to Dashboard → My Team and create your team with a name and game.

3

Add Players

Add up to 5+ players to your roster using their usernames.

4

Register for Tournament

Browse tournaments and click "Register Team" on any open tournament.

5

Compete!

Wait for admin approval, then check your match schedule and play!
